Your day stays yours.

Dayweave is a hackathon prototype that helps you plan sleep, coffee, and meals around tomorrow’s calendar. Updated September 13, 2026.

Demo and connected use

Demo mode uses fictional receipts and events. It does not access Google accounts. In connected mode, Google asks you to authorize Gmail receipt reading, access to events on calendars you own, and access to a spreadsheet created by this app.

Where information goes

Google access tokens stay in this page’s memory. Your Google password is never available to Dayweave. API requests go directly from your browser to Google. Dayweave has no application server that collects your mailbox, calendar, or credentials.

If you enter a Gemini API key and enable AI processing, the app sends minimized structured planning context to Google’s Gemini API. Review the consent text before enabling this. Gemini is governed by Google’s applicable API terms and data-handling policies. Your key stays in page memory and is cleared when the page is closed or reloaded. Do not use sensitive health information in this prototype.

What is saved

Local browser storage may hold your preferences, the app-created spreadsheet reference, public OAuth client ID, and clearly labeled demo state. Google credentials and Gemini keys are not saved in browser storage. Saving preferences and applying actions are explicit operations. Approved reminders are stored in your Google Calendar; the app-created Sheet stores preferences and action history.

Your control

Generating a plan does not create calendar events. Review and approve selected actions before they are applied. Disconnect to clear the current Google session from the app. You can revoke its access from Google Account connections and remove created events or the spreadsheet in Google’s own apps. Clearing this site’s browser storage removes its local saved settings.

Limits

Receipts show purchases, not consumption. A calendar shows scheduled commitments, not actual sleep, energy expenditure, or dietary needs. Suggestions are planning aids based on your selected preferences, not medical or nutrition treatment.

Report an issue without including keys, private messages, health information, or other personal data.
